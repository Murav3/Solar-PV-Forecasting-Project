1. Inductive Conformal Prediction (ICP) Safety Ribbon
"Sir, point predictions are clinically inadequate for mission-critical power grids because error distributions during cloud fluctuations are non-Gaussian and heavy-tailed. To solve this, we implemented Inductive Conformal Prediction (ICP). Unlike traditional confidence intervals that blindly assume Gaussian bell curves, Conformal Prediction provides distribution-free, finite-sample coverage guarantees at a user-defined 1 - α = 90% or 95% significance level, giving grid dispatchers provably bounded operating reserves."
Formula: P(Y_t ∈ [L_t, U_t]) ≥ 1 - α Quantile: q̂ = Quantile_{(⌈(n+1)(1-α)⌉/n)}(|e_i|/s_i) Scaling: s_t Diurnal Solar Envelope
2. Grid Ramp-Rate Criticality Monitor (dP/dt) & BESS Dispatch
"Sir, in utility power systems, the primary trigger of rolling blackouts isn't the volume of power generated, but the Ramp Rate: the first derivative of power with respect to time (dP/dt). We engineered an automated Ramp-Rate Criticality Monitor adhering to NERC BAL-002 standards. If the aggregate fleet drop exceeds 25 MW/min, the platform triggers an automated spinning-reserve alert, specifying the exact megawatt capacity of fast-response battery storage required to preserve grid frequency stability."
Formula: dP/dt = ΔP / Δt (MW/min) BESS Dispatch: P_BESS = |dP/dt_drop| × Δt Standards: NERC BAL-002, CAISO FRP
